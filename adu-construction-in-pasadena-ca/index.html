Why build an ADU

More usable space on the lot you already own

Add value and space to your property with Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), perfect for guests, rentals, or extended family.

An ADU is a full project in miniature: foundation, framing, roofing, plumbing, electrical service and finishes all have to line up. Hillside General Construction & Electrical handles the construction and the electrical work with the same team, so the panel capacity, circuits and lighting are planned alongside the build instead of bolted on at the end.

  • A separate, self-contained living space for guests, rentals or extended family
  • Construction and electrical delivered by one contractor
  • Electrical service and panel capacity assessed as part of the build
  • Finished with the meticulous attention to detail we apply to every residential project
