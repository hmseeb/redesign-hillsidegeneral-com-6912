Construction services

Every construction need, one team

From a single bathroom to a full facility, these are the services our crews deliver across Pasadena, Glendale, Burbank and the wider Los Angeles County area.

  • 01 General contracting

    One point of contact who plans the work, coordinates the trades and keeps your project moving from permit to punch list.

  • 02 ADU construction

    Add value and space to your property with Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), perfect for guests, rentals, or extended family.

  • 03 Kitchen remodeling

    Reworked layouts, new cabinetry, surfaces, lighting and power so the busiest room in the house finally fits how you cook and live.

  • 04 Bathroom remodeling

    Full bathroom upgrades — tile, fixtures, waterproofing, ventilation and electrical — finished with meticulous attention to detail.

  • 05 Kitchen & bathroom cabinetry

    Cabinetry built and installed to suit your space, your storage and the finish you have chosen for the rest of the room.

  • 06 Balcony construction

    New balconies and decks designed and built as structure first, so the space you relax on is sound underneath.

  • 07 Balcony inspections & repairs

    Inspection and repair of existing balconies and elevated exterior elements, with the repairs carried out by the same team.

  • 08 Fire rebuild

    Rebuilding homes and buildings after fire damage — structure, systems and finishes brought back to current code.

  • 09 Retrofit

    Seismic upgrades, energy efficiency improvements, structural reinforcements and ADA compliance modifications.

  • 10 Hardscape construction

    Driveways, patios, pools, water features and firepits that turn the ground around your property into usable outdoor space.

  • 11 Private label construction

    Construction capacity delivered behind the scenes for designers, developers and firms who need a build partner they can rely on.
